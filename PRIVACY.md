# Privacy Notice

This application keeps a log of your readings (time, pulse, the raw measurement, and the activity, duration and validity you tag them with) in the app's private storage on your device. It doesn't send it to any third party. If Android's backup is enabled on your device, the system may include this log in your device backup.

The picture of the camera is not recorded, and only its aggregate information (sum of its pixel values) are being analyzed.

The data generated by the application doesn't leave the device, unless explicitly shared by the User. The accelerometer is read only while the app is open, to suggest the activity; its values are not recorded, except for the direction of gravity at the start of each reading, which is stored in the log.
